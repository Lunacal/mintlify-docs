## What it does

With Subscriptions turned on for an event type:

- Your client pays **when they first subscribe**, then gets **charged automatically** every cycle after that — no reminders or manual invoices needed from you.
- Each time they're billed, they get a fresh batch of sessions to book (you decide how many).
- Everything runs through **Stripe**, so payments, retries, and receipts are handled for you.

This is a great fit for coaching retainers, memberships, recurring lessons, or any "pay monthly, book as you go" setup.

## Before you turn it on

A few things need to be true first:

- **You need Stripe connected** as your payment method — Subscriptions doesn't work with PayPal or free events.
- **You need a price set** under "Charge for this event" — it has to be more than \$0.
- Your event type **can't** be a collective event (multiple hosts sharing bookings).
- Your event type **can only have one duration option** — Subscriptions doesn't work with multiple session lengths.
- You **can't** combine it with a flexible-count package or with deposit payments — turning Subscriptions on will automatically switch deposits off.

If any of these aren't met, you'll see a message explaining what to fix when you try to enable it.

> This feature is currently in **Beta**.

## Setting it up

1. Go to the event type → **Payments** tab.
2. Make sure **Stripe** is selected and a price is set.
3. Turn on **Enable subscription (autopay)**.
4. Choose your **billing frequency** — how often to charge (every 1 day, 2 weeks, 1 month, 1 year, etc.).
5. Set **sessions per billing cycle** — how many bookings each payment unlocks (1 to 20). If you leave it at 1, your client gets one session per payment.
6. Save.

That's it — your event's booking page will now show it as a subscription instead of a one-time booking.

**Note:** while Subscriptions is on, the separate Packages & Recurring tab is hidden, since session counts are configured right here instead.

## What your client sees

When they visit your booking page:

- The price is shown per cycle, like `$49 / month`.
- They'll see a note that it renews automatically and how many sessions they get each time.
- Instead of "Book," the button says **Subscribe**.

At checkout, they're told clearly that this is a recurring charge and that it continues automatically until they cancel.

Once they pay:

- Their subscription becomes active.
- Their first batch of sessions is unlocked immediately, and they can start booking.

## Coupons

You can apply a discount code to a subscription, and choose whether it applies:

- **First payment only** — full price kicks back in from the second cycle onward, or
- **Every payment** — the discount applies for as long as they stay subscribed.

One limit: a **100% off** coupon can't be used on a subscription — some discount has to still leave a charge.

## Renewals

Every time the billing cycle repeats:

- Stripe charges your client automatically.
- If the payment goes through, they get their next batch of sessions right away, and may get a "sessions ready to book" email.
- **Unused sessions from the previous cycle don't roll over** — it's a fresh allotment each time, so let your clients know this if they tend to book less than what they're allotted.

We also send your client a heads-up before they're charged again:

| Billing frequency | Reminder sent |
| --- | --- |
| Weekly | 1 day before renewal |
| Monthly | 3 days before renewal |
| Yearly | 7 days before renewal |
| Daily | No advance reminder |

## If a payment fails

If a charge doesn't go through:

- The subscription shows as **Payment retrying**.
- No new sessions are added until it's resolved.
- Your client gets an email letting them know, with a link to update their payment method.
- Stripe automatically retries the charge over the next few days.

If it still doesn't go through after retries, the subscription moves to **Paused** — no more charge attempts, and no new sessions, until they resubscribe or fix it.

## Cancelling

Either you or your client can cancel a subscription. Either way:

- **Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period** — not immediately.
- Your client keeps their remaining sessions and access until then.
- They won't be charged again.

If someone wants to come back later, they'll see a **Subscribe again** option.

## Managing subscriptions

**As a host:** Go to your **Bookings** list → **Subscriptions** tab. There you can see each subscriber's status, when they renew or end, sessions left, total paid, and full payment history — and cancel a subscription from there if needed.

**As a client:** They manage everything from Client Portal`/package/subscriptions` in their account — an **Active** tab and an **Inactive** tab, showing sessions left, next renewal date, saved payment method, payment history, and the option to cancel or resubscribe.

## Common questions

**Why can't I turn autopay on?** Check that: Stripe is your payment method, you've set a price above \$0, the event isn't collective, it only has one duration, and you're not using a flexible-count package. Also make sure the event type isn't locked by a team admin.

**What's the difference between Packages and Subscriptions?** Packages are a one-time upfront payment for a set (or flexible) number of sessions. Subscriptions charge automatically on a repeating schedule and refresh the session count each cycle. You can't use both at once.

**Do unused sessions carry over to the next cycle?** No — each renewal gives a fresh batch, regardless of what was left over.

**Can my client cancel mid-cycle and get a refund?** They can cancel anytime, but it only takes effect at the end of the period they've already paid for — they keep access until then rather than losing it immediately. Refunds aren't handled automatically as part of cancelling.

**Can I accept PayPal for a subscription?** No — Stripe only.

**Can I use a 100%-off coupon?** No, subscriptions require some payment to go through each cycle.

**What's the maximum sessions per cycle?** 20 per cycle. Clients can book up to 10 slots in a single booking action.

**Where do I see my subscribers?** Bookings → **Subscriptions** tab.

**Where does my client manage their subscription?** `/package/subscriptions` in their account, under Active or Inactive.

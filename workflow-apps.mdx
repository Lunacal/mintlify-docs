When you add an app to a workflow, Lunacal asks for one value from that app, such as a channel, a list, or a page. Connecting the app grants access. It does not choose where the booking goes.

Pick the app below and copy the value it names. Lunacal fills the meeting time, the attendee, and the meeting link on its own when that app has a field for them.

A cancellation updates the record from the original booking. It does not create a second task, page, ticket, or subscriber.

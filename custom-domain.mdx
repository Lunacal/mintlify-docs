## What Custom Domain does

Custom Domain lets you use **your own branded URL** for scheduling pages instead of only the default Lunacal link. **Example** <br />`book.mydomain.com/jeff` instead of a Lunacal-hosted link like `lunacal.ai/jeff`. Your existing Lunacal booking links continue to work. A custom domain is an **additional** branded way for people to reach the same scheduling experience.

## Pricing and purchase rules

Where to buy: **Settings → Custom Domain** (`Buy Domain`).

### Current purchase terms (as shown in the purchase dialog)

- **Price:** **\$200** per domain (lifetime / one-time payment)
- **Buy now, activate anytime in future**
- **One-time payment, no renewals**
- **Domains cannot be reassigned**
- **This purchase is non-refundable** You can choose how many domains to buy in one checkout (up to the number of slots still available under your limit). Checkout is completed through Stripe. After a successful purchase, the app instructs you to click **Activate a domain** in the domains list to begin setup.

### Domain quota (limits)

Your account has:

- **Bought** — how many custom domain slots you have purchased
- **Limit** — the maximum you are allowed to purchase<br />The list header shows unused slots in this form: **“X of Y domains unused.”** **Quota rules (shown under Rules in Settings):**

1. Only domains **you own** count toward your quota. If someone else owns a domain and invites you to their team, that does **not** use your quota.
2. When you assign a domain to a team, every member gets their own booking page on that domain (e.g. `book.yourdomain.com/person`) **in addition to** their `lunacal.ai/person` link.
3. Adding more team members does **not** use extra domain slots. Ten people on the same domain still count as **1** toward the owner’s limit.
4. Domain purchases are **non-refundable**. **Default Lunacal domain:** Your account always includes the Lunacal-hosted domain (shown as owned by Lunacal). That is separate from purchased custom domains. **Pro / Enterprise:** Purchases are capped by the subscription domain limit (product default maximum is **5** for Pro-tier scheduling). **AppSumo (by tier configuration):**

| Tier | Can buy? | Included / limit context |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tier 1 | No | No custom domain purchase |
| Tier 2 | Yes | Higher purchase ceiling than Tier 1 |
| Tier 3 | Yes | Higher ceiling than Tier 2 |
| Tier 4 | Yes | Highest AppSumo ceiling |

Exact remaining count is always shown in the purchase dialog (“X domain(s) left”).

## Host setup: Activate a domain (3 steps)

Prerequisite: at least one **unused purchased** domain slot.

Click **Activate a domain**. The wizard is titled **“Add your custom domain”** (Beta).

### Step 1 — Enter Domain

Hosts choose:

1. **Subdomain** — selected from an allowed list (unless Lunacal has specially enabled free-form entry for that account)
2. **Root Domain** — their own domain, e.g. `yourcompany.com`

**Preview shown:** `https://{subdomain}.{rootdomain}`

Hosts need access to DNS at their registrar (examples mentioned in product: GoDaddy, Namecheap, Cloudflare).

**Allowed subdomain prefixes (standard list):**

`agenda`, `appointments`, `avtale`, `beacon`, `book`, `booking`, `bookings`, `bookme`, `booknow`, `cal`, `calendar`, `calendario`, `calender`, `chat`, `chet`, `chiamata`, `coffee`, `connect`, `consult`, `contact`, `demo`, `go`, `kalender`, `letstalk`, `luna`, `me`, `meet`, `meeting`, `meetings`, `procalendar`, `rdv`, `reserver`, `reservas`, `schedule`, `support`, `termin`, `termine`, `tutoring`, `tutors`, `ventas`, `www`

If the desired subdomain is not listed, the product tells hosts to email [**hi@lunacal.live**](mailto:hi@lunacal.live).

**Format rules enforced at setup:**

- Subdomain: lowercase letters, numbers, hyphens (valid hostname label)
- Root domain: valid domain format (e.g. `example.com`)
- Full address must not already be in use by another Lunacal account

After Continue, the app provisions routing and generates DNS records. Progress messages include registering the domain, provisioning CDN, configuring routing, and finalizing. Hosts are asked **not to close the window** during that step.

Once past Step 1, the domain **cannot be changed** in that setup flow.

### Step 2 — Configure DNS

Hosts must add **two DNS records** at their domain provider:

#### 1) CNAME

| Field | What to use |
| :-- | :-- |
| Type | **CNAME** |
| Host / Name | The subdomain (e.g. `book`) |
| Value / Target | The value Lunacal shows (points the subdomain to Lunacal’s servers) |
| Purpose | Points your subdomain to Lunacal |

#### 2) TXT (ownership verification)

| Field | What to use |
| :-- | :-- |
| Type | **TXT** |
| Host / Name | `_dnsauth.{subdomain}` (e.g. `_dnsauth.book`) |
| Value / Target | The validation token Lunacal shows |
| Purpose | Verifies domain ownership |

Both Host and Value can be copied from the setup UI.

**Cloudflare tip (shown in product):** Keep the proxy **disabled** (grey cloud / DNS only).

**Timing:** CDN propagation can take up to **40 minutes to 1 hour**. Hosts can close the window and return later.

**Verify DNS** checks whether records are correct. Status may still be pending; the product may say verification can take up to **1 hour**.

If validation **times out**, hosts can **Regen**erate the TXT token and update DNS with the new value.

If setup was left incomplete for a long time, continuing setup may refresh DNS values. Hosts must replace old CNAME/TXT values with the new ones shown.

After DNS and CDN are approved, hosts continue to Step 3.

### Step 3 — Activate

When ready, the UI shows:

- Preview of `https://{subdomain}.{domain}`
- **DNS verified**
- **CDN configured**

Host clicks **Activate Domain**.

**After activation (product messaging):**

- Global propagation can take **45 minutes – 2 hours**
- A **403** or Azure-related error during that window can be normal
- If issues persist after **24 hours**, email [**hi@lunacal.live**](mailto:hi@lunacal.live)

HTTPS certificates and global CDN are set up automatically (hosts do not upload certificates).

## Host settings after the domain is Live

### 1) Make Primary

Available for:

- The default Lunacal domain (when it is not already primary), or  
- An **active** custom domain

**Primary** means: this is the main booking link shown in the **dashboard header**.

If you activate a custom domain and you did not already have a primary custom default set, that new domain can become your default automatically.

### 2) Favicon (browser tab icon)

On domains **you own**, click the favicon area next to the domain name to upload or change a favicon.

- Image files only  
- Max size **1 MB**  
- Cropped square in the image editor

Domains that are only “Part of team” show the favicon but do not allow you to change it.

Attendees visiting pages on that custom domain see this favicon in the browser tab (when a favicon is set for that domain).

### 3) Set Redirect (root of the custom domain)

For an **active, user-owned** custom domain, **Set Redirect** chooses where visitors go when they open the **domain root** (e.g. visiting `https://book.yourcompany.com` with no path).

**Options:**

| Option | What happens for the visitor |
| :-- | :-- |
| **No Redirect** | Default domain page behavior (falls back to the owner’s username page when no redirect path is set) |
| **Team Pages** (team owners) | Redirect to a team page, e.g. `/team/{team-slug}` |
| **Team Events** (team owners) | Redirect to a specific team event, e.g. `/team/{team-slug}/{event-slug}` |
| **Member / your pages** | Redirect to a person’s page `/{username}` or a specific event `/{username}/{event-slug}` |

Team owners see all relevant members and team events. Non-owners typically see their own pages under “Your Pages.”

The dialog shows the current target as `{hostname}{path}` (or “No redirect set…”).

### 4) View Records

For live custom domains, hosts can reopen the CNAME and TXT values that were used for setup (useful for DNS audits or recreating records).

### 5) Assign domain to a team

In **Team Settings**, owners can assign a custom domain (or the Lunacal default) to the team from a dropdown of domains they own.

**Effect:** Team members get booking pages on that domain, for example:

- Team: `{custom-domain}/team/{team-slug}`
- Person: `{custom-domain}/{username}`<br />while `lunacal.ai/...` links still work.

### 6) Delete Domain

Owners can delete a domain they own from the domain list menu.

**Before deleting, hosts must:**

1. Remove the **CNAME** and **TXT** records from their DNS provider
2. Confirm the checkbox: “I have deleted the CNAME and TXT records from my DNS”

**After delete:**

- The domain is removed from Lunacal  
- The purchased slot becomes available to activate a different (or later the same) domain again  
- Global cleanup can take time — wait before reusing the **same** domain name  
- If that domain was Primary, primary falls back (default subdomain cleared)

Failed setups use a similar “Remove Failed Domain” flow to free the slot.

The Rules dialog also mentions emailing [**hi@lunacal.live**](mailto:hi@lunacal.live) with subject **“Remove my custom domain.”** In-app delete is available for owned domains; email remains a support path if needed.

## What attendees (guests) see

Attendees do **not** see the Custom Domain settings screen. They only experience the branded URLs and pages.

### URLs attendees may use

| Link type | Example shape | Attendee experience |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Personal booking page | `https://book.yourcompany.com/alex` | Same scheduling profile/theme as Lunacal, on your domain |
| Specific event | `https://book.yourcompany.com/alex/30min` | Event booking page on your domain |
| Team page | `https://book.yourcompany.com/team/sales` | Team booking page on your domain |
| Team event | `https://book.yourcompany.com/team/sales/demo` | Team event on your domain |
| Domain root | `https://book.yourcompany.com` | Redirects based on host’s **Set Redirect** (or default to owner’s username page if none set) |
| Original Lunacal link | `lunacal.ai/alex` (or equivalent Lunacal host) | Still works; not replaced |

### Branding attendees notice

- Browser address bar shows **your domain** (HTTPS)
- Browser tab can show **your favicon** (if the host uploaded one for that domain)
- Content is the host’s Lunacal booking / scheduling pages (profile, events, availability, booking flow)

### During propagation (important for support)

Shortly after activation, attendees might briefly see errors (including **403**). Product guidance: this can be normal for up to **24 hours** while the domain finishes propagating worldwide.

If a custom domain is **not active**, those branded URLs are not served as live booking pages.

## Common support fixes

| Customer question | Guidance |
| :-- | :-- |
| “I bought a domain but nothing is live” | Purchase only adds a **slot**. They must click **Activate a domain** and finish DNS + Activate. |
| “Activate is greyed out” | No unused slots, or activation temporarily unavailable (contact [**hi@lunacal.ai**](mailto:hi@lunacal.ai) if the tooltip says so). |
| “My subdomain isn’t in the list” | Email [**hi@lunacal.live**](mailto:hi@lunacal.live) — standard accounts choose from the allowed list only. |
| “DNS isn’t verifying” | Confirm CNAME + TXT exact values; Cloudflare must be **DNS only** (proxy off); wait up to ~1 hour; try Verify again; Regen TXT if timed out. |
| “I see 403 after Activate” | Normal during global propagation (45 min–2 hours; up to 24 hours). Contact [**hi@lunacal.live**](mailto:hi@lunacal.live) if still broken after 24 hours. |
| “Can I put this on my bare domain (example.com) without a subdomain?” | Setup is **subdomain + root domain** (e.g. `book.example.com`). `www` is an allowed subdomain prefix. |
| “Does buying remove lunacal.ai?” | No. Lunacal links remain. Custom domain is additional. |
| “Do team members each need to buy a domain?” | No. Owner assigns one domain to the team; members get pages on it without using their own quota. |
| “Can I transfer my purchased domain slot to another account?” | Product states domains **cannot be reassigned**; purchases are **non-refundable**. |
| “How do I change where book.mydomain.com goes?” | Use **Set Redirect** on that live domain. |
| “How do I change the browser icon?” | Upload favicon on an owned domain in the domain list. |
| “I want to remove a domain” | Prefer in-app **Delete Domain** (remove DNS first). Or email [**hi@lunacal.live**](mailto:hi@lunacal.live) with subject “Remove my custom domain.” |

## Industry & persona use cases

These use cases map **existing** Custom Domain capabilities (branded subdomain URL, primary link, favicon, root redirect, team assignment, multi-domain slots) to how different customers typically apply them. They do not add product features beyond what is listed above.

### Founder / startup operator

- Use `book.yourstartup.com` or `demo.yourstartup.com` as the Primary link in the dashboard and in decks / LinkedIn.
- Set root redirect to a flagship event (e.g. “Founder intro” or “Product demo”).
- Keep `lunacal.ai` as backup while pitching; share the branded link externally.

### Sales team

- Assign one purchased domain to the sales **team** so every rep has `meet.company.com/{rep}` plus team pages like `meet.company.com/team/sales`.
- Redirect the domain root to the team page or a shared “Book a demo” team event.
- Use one domain slot for the whole team (extra seats do not consume extra domain slots).

### Professional services (consultants, lawyers, accountants, agencies of record)

- Brand discovery calls as `consult.firmname.com` or `appointments.firmname.com`.
- Upload a firm favicon so the tab matches the website.
- Redirect the root to the principal’s page or a “New client consult” event.
- Optionally buy a second domain slot for a practice area (e.g. `support.firmname.com`) if quota allows.

### Healthcare / wellness practitioners

- Share `appointments.clinicname.com/{provider}` for a more clinical, trustworthy URL.
- Team assignment for multi-provider practices so each clinician has a path on the same domain.
- Root redirect to a general “New patient” or front-desk team event.
- Favicon aligned with clinic branding for patient confidence in the browser tab.

### Coach / tutor / educator

- Use friendly prefixes from the allowed list: `book`, `booking`, `tutoring`, `tutors`, `coffee`, `letstalk`, `meet`.
- Primary link for social bios and course platforms.
- Redirect root to the most common session type (e.g. discovery call).

### Agency (marketing, creative, recruiting)

- One agency-owned domain assigned to client-facing teams: `book.agency.com/team/{pod}`.
- Separate purchased domains (within limit) for major brands or regions if needed.
- Root redirect to a routing / intake team page; individual strategists still get `book.agency.com/{name}`.

### Spa and salon

- Use `book.spname.com` or `appointments.spname.com` on Google Business, Instagram, and booking cards.
- Team domain so each stylist/therapist has a personal path without separate purchases.
- Redirect root to a “Choose a service” team page or featured treatment event.
- Favicon matching the salon site for a consistent guest journey from website → book.

### Real estate

- Agents: `meet.brokerage.com/{agent}` for listing consultations and buyer calls.
- Brokerage owner assigns one domain to the team; agents keep Lunacal links as fallback.
- Redirect root to a general “Speak with an agent” team event or office page.
- Useful prefixes: `meet`, `meeting`, `book`, `contact`, `schedule`.

### Small businesses (local services, freelancers, studios)

- Replace generic links in email signatures and invoices with `schedule.yourbusiness.com` (or `book` / `booking`).
- Single domain is usually enough; set Primary and one redirect to the main offer.
- Keep Lunacal default domain as Primary only until custom domain is Live, then switch Primary.

### Cross-cutting patterns that work for almost any industry

1. **One brand domain for the public; Lunacal domain as always-on backup**
2. **Team assignment when multiple people book under one brand**
3. **Root redirect as the “front door”** (team page or flagship event)
4. **Favicon** for polish when the domain is customer-facing
5. **Extra purchased slots** only when you truly need separate brand hostnames (within plan limit)

## Quick reference: host checklist

1. Be on **Pro / Enterprise** or **AppSumo Tier 2+**
2. **Buy Domain** (\$200 lifetime per slot; non-refundable)
3. Click **Activate a domain**
4. Pick allowed **subdomain** + enter **root domain**
5. Add **CNAME** and **TXT** at DNS (Cloudflare: DNS only)
6. **Verify DNS** → wait if pending → continue when approved
7. **Activate Domain**
8. Wait for propagation (up to 24 hours if errors appear)
9. Optionally: **Make Primary**, upload **favicon**, **Set Redirect**, assign to **team**
10. Share `{your-domain}/{username}` (and team paths) with attendees

## Use Cases

> Use this guide if you want to manage scheduling for more than one business or client under a single Lunacal account.

With **Workspaces**, each company (or client) gets its own **Team Workspace** — with separate members, event types, bookings, booking pages, integrations, and settings.

Common use cases:

- You own multiple independent companies that need separate scheduling.
- You run an agency and manage scheduling for multiple clients.
- You’re a consultant or freelancer working across several companies.

> Every Lunacal account starts with a **Personal Workspace**. Each additional company is a **Team Workspace**. Switch between them anytime from the workspace dropdown in the top-left of your dashboard.

## This help doc is divided into 4 sections

1. **Section 1:** Create a Team Workspace for Company 1 and invite members
2. **Section 2:** Members accept the invite and join the workspace
3. **Section 3:** Repeat for Company 2 (and any more companies)
4. **Section 4:** Set up events and booking pages for each company

---

## Section 1: Create a Team Workspace and Invite Members

### Step 1

Open the **workspace dropdown** in the top-left of your dashboard, then click **Create Team**.

<Frame>
  ![Ifyhmt1](/images/ifyhmt1-1.webp "Ifyhmt1")
</Frame>

### Step 2

Enter:

- **Team name** \[e.g. `Company 1`\]
- **Team URL** (slug) — this becomes part of the public team booking link
- **Timezone**

Then click **Continue**.

<Frame>
  ![Ifyhmt2](/images/ifyhmt2.webp "Ifyhmt2")
</Frame>

### Step 3

You can invite members now, or finish setup and invite them later from **Team Members**.

> Switch into that Team Workspace (workspace dropdown).

To invite:

1. Enter email(s), choose a role (**Admin** or **Member**), and send the invite.

<Frame>
  ![](/images/company-setup/CT3.webp)
</Frame>

**Note:** Each invited member uses **1 seat** on your plan (extra seats are billed on your subscription). Make sure your plan has enough seats before inviting multiple people.

After creation:

- You become the **Owner** of that workspace.
- That company’s events, bookings, members, and settings stay isolated from your other workspaces.

---

## Section 2: Members Accept the Invite and Join

### Step 4

Invited members get an email from Lunacal to join the team.

<Frame>
  ![](/images/company-setup/CT4.webp)
</Frame>

### Step 5

If they don’t have a Lunacal account yet, they click **Create your account** (or **Accept Invitation**) and complete sign-up.

If they already have an account, they sign in with that email.

<Frame>
  ![](/images/company-setup/CT5.webp)
</Frame>

### Step 6

After signing up or logging in, they **accepted the pending invite** in the app, if not:

1. Open the **workspace dropdown** (top-left).
2. Under **Your Teams**, find the pending invite (it shows as **Pending Invite**).
3. Click the green **Accept** checkmark (or decline with the X).

<Frame>
  ![Screenshot](/images/Screenshot-5.webp "Screenshot")
</Frame>

> Important: Creating an account alone does **not** join the team. They must Accept the invite from the workspace dropdown.

Once accepted:

- They get access to that Team Workspace under your plan (no separate purchase needed).
- They also keep their own **Personal Workspace** for independent scheduling.

For a full new-member checklist (profile, calendar, availability), see [Team Members Onboarding](https://help.lunacal.ai/team-members-onboarding).

### Step 7

You’ll now have:

- Your **Personal Workspace**
- One **Team Workspace**

Switch between companies anytime from the workspace dropdown. When you switch, the whole dashboard (Event Types, Bookings, Team Members, Booking Page, Integrations, Settings) shows that company’s data only.

## Section 4: Create Events and Booking Pages for Each Company

Switch into the correct Team Workspace first, then configure scheduling for that company only.

### What you can set up per company

| Area | What to do |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Event Types** | Create team events: Managed (1:1), Round Robin, or Collective |
| **Team Members** | Invite people, set roles, assign events, set availability |
| **Booking Page** | Customize the team booking page and member pages |
| **Integrations** | Connect calendars, video, and other apps for the workspace |
| **Settings** | Branding, SEO, privacy, team availability, workflows, custom domain |

### Setup patterns

**Solo professional in a company workspace (e.g. one doctor)**

1. Invite them as a member.
2. Create a [**Managed Event**](https://help.lunacal.ai/workspace/managed-event) and assigned to them).

**Multi-person company (e.g. a salon with several barbers)**

1. Invite all staff as members.
2. Create [**Managed Events**](https://help.lunacal.ai/workspace/managed-event) so you control settings centrally and assign them to staff.
3. Optionally create a [**Team Booking Page**](https://help.lunacal.ai/workspace/edit-team-booking-page) so clients pick a person (or a service).
4. Use [**Round Robin**](https://help.lunacal.ai/workspace/manage-event-types) if you want Lunacal to auto-assign the next available person.
5. Use [**Collective**](https://help.lunacal.ai/workspace/manage-event-types) only when everyone must attend the same meeting.

You do **not** need each member to recreate events manually. As Owner/Admin, you can manage events and assignments from the Team Workspace.

**Important:** Every member you add consumes a seat. Seats cover their access under your plan; they don’t need a separate Lunacal subscription for that team.

## Example

You run an agency with 2 clients:

- **New Age Salon** — 4 barbers (Jeff, Linda, Tara, Amy)
- **Dr. Dave Matthews** — solo doctor

### Setup

1. Create **Team Workspace: New Age Salon** → invite Jeff, Linda, Tara, Amy → **4 seats**.
2. Create **Team Workspace: Dr. Dave** → invite Dr. Dave → **1 seat**.

**Total = 5 seats** (plus your own account as Owner, depending on how seats are counted on your plan).

### How it works

- Salon members cannot see Dr. Dave’s workspace data, and vice versa.
- Switching the workspace dropdown changes which company’s dashboard you see.
- When members accept, they get paid access under your plan.

**For Dr. Dave**

- Create a Managed Event (or simple team event) assigned to him.
- Share his booking link or the team booking page. Round Robin usually isn’t needed for one person.

**For New Age Salon**

- Create Managed Events for services and assign the right barbers.
- Publish a **Team Booking Page** so clients can choose a barber.
- Or create a **Round Robin** event so Lunacal assigns a barber automatically.

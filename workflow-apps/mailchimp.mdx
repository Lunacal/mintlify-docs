A Mailchimp step adds the person who booked to one audience. The Audience ID is on that audience's settings page. It is not the audience name.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the audience">
    In Mailchimp, open **Audience**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the settings">
    Choose **Manage Audience**, then **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the Audience ID">
    On **Audience name and defaults**, copy **Audience ID**. Paste that value into **Audience ID** in the Lunacal workflow.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Email** must be one address. It starts as the attendee's email. If you insert another variable beside it, Lunacal keeps the address and drops the extra text. Put the name in **First name** and **Last name** instead. Those are saved on the subscriber.

**Status when booked** is `Subscribed` unless you choose **Pending confirmation**. If the booking is later cancelled, Lunacal sets that same person to unsubscribed. It does not add a second subscriber.

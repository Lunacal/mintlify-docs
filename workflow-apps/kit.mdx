A Kit step adds the person who booked as a subscriber. Kit does not ask for an audience ID or a list ID. Lunacal sends the attendee's email and first name, and marks the subscriber active.

**Email** must be one address. It starts as the attendee's email. Extra text typed beside that address is removed.

If the same email books again, Kit updates that subscriber's first name. Kit's update action has no inactive state, so a cancellation does not unsubscribe them.

A Slack step posts one message in the channel you name. Paste the channel name, or its ID, with no `#` in front.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the channel">
    In Slack, open the channel that should receive booking messages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the name or the ID">
    Use the channel name without `#`. For a channel shown as `#general`, paste `general`.

    To use the ID instead, click the channel name at the top of Slack and copy the channel ID.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it into the workflow">
    In Lunacal, open the workflow, choose Slack, and paste the value into **Channel**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The message includes the event name, the attendee, the attendee email, the meeting time, and the meeting link when the booking has one.

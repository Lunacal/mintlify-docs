title: "Open a Zendesk ticket from a booking"
sidebarTitle: "Zendesk"
description: "Open one Zendesk ticket when someone books, and mark that same ticket solved when the booking is cancelled instead of opening another."
keywords:

---

A Zendesk step opens one support ticket when someone books. You do not paste a ticket ID or a brand ID. The subject, description, requester email, and requester name are filled from the booking, and you can edit the wording.

The description includes the attendee, the meeting time, and the meeting link. **Requester email** must stay a single email address.

If that booking is cancelled, Lunacal marks the same ticket solved and adds a private note. It does not open a second ticket. If the booking is rescheduled, Lunacal adds a private note with the new time on that same ticket.

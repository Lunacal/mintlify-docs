## What are buffers and booking limits?

**Meeting buffer time** is time reserved before or after an appointment for preparation, notes, or a break.

**Booking limits** are rules that restrict when or how often appointments can be scheduled. They can set a minimum notice period, cap repeat bookings, or limit how far ahead someone can book.

In LunaCal’s appointment booking software, configure these controls under **Event Types → select an event → Booking Limits**.

## Which booking setting should I use?

| What you want to control | Setting |
| --- | --- |
| Meeting buffer time before or after appointments | Buffer Before / Buffer After |
| Minimum booking notice to prevent last-minute bookings | Advance Notice |
| Booking frequency per person | Max Bookings per Event |
| How far ahead attendees can book | Advance Booking Limit |

Configure only the options you need. Buffer time, advance notice, and the advance booking limit are optional; leaving them at their default values does not prevent the event from working. Booking limits and buffers work alongside your availability schedule, connected calendars, and [meeting duration](/event-types/meeting-duration).

## How to set buffers and booking limits in LunaCal

### Select the event type

Open [Event Types](https://app.lunacal.ai/event-types) and select the event you want to edit. Click the event card or use its gear icon.

  ![Select the event type to configure in LunaCal](/images/limits_and_buffer/1.webp)
</Frame>

### Open Booking Limits

Click **Booking Limits** to configure the event’s booking rules.

<Frame>
  ![Open Booking Limits for the selected event](/images/limits_and_buffer/2.webp)
</Frame>

### Add a buffer before meetings

In **Booking Limits**, set **Buffer Before** to leave preparation time before a meeting. Before- and after-meeting buffers each support up to **120 minutes**.

**Recommended starting value:** 10 minutes before the meeting.

**Example:** Before a demo, add a 10-minute buffer to review notes and open your presentation.

<Frame>
  ![Configure the buffer before a meeting](/images/limits_and_buffer/3.webp)
</Frame>

### Add a buffer after meetings

Set **Buffer After** to leave time after the meeting and avoid back-to-back calls.

**Recommended starting value:** 15 minutes after the meeting.

**Example:** After a sales call, allow 15–20 minutes to write follow-up points and prepare for your next appointment.

<Frame>
  ![Configure the buffer after a meeting](/images/limits_and_buffer/4.webp)
</Frame>

### Set minimum booking notice

Under **Advance Notice**, set the minimum booking notice: the lead time required between making a booking and the appointment starting. Choose minutes, hours, or days to prevent last-minute bookings.

**Recommended starting value:** 2 hours.

**Example:** Require at least 2 hours’ notice so attendees cannot book a meeting that starts immediately.

<Frame>
  ![Set the minimum advance notice for bookings](/images/limits_and_buffer/5.webp)
</Frame>

### Limit bookings per person

Use **Max Bookings per Event** to control how often the same person can book this event within a day, week, month, or year. You can add up to **four** limit rules for an event.

**Example:** For coaching, limit each person to two sessions per week to manage booking frequency.

<Frame>
  ![Set booking frequency limits per person](/images/limits_and_buffer/6.webp)
</Frame>

### Set how far ahead people can book

Use **Advance Booking Limit** to control how far into the future attendees can see and book appointments. Allow bookings within a window or specify the exact days you want open.

**Recommended starting value:** 30 days.

**Example:** Show only the next 30 days so attendees cannot book months ahead.

Changes to **Advance Booking Limit** and **Advance Notice** apply to new bookings. Existing bookings remain unchanged.

<Frame>
  ![Set the advance booking window](/images/limits_and_buffer/7.webp)
</Frame>

### Save your settings

Click **Save** to apply your booking limits and buffers.

  ![Save the booking limits and buffer settings](/images/limits_and_buffer/8.webp)

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Forgetting to save, which leaves the previous settings active.
- Setting buffers but leaving advance notice at zero, which still permits last-minute bookings.
- Adding overlapping frequency limits, such as one booking per day and one per 24 hours, that complicate availability.
- Setting a short advance booking window and expecting later dates to remain available.

## Suggested settings to start with

- **Buffers:** 10 minutes before and 15 minutes after meetings.
- **Advance notice:** 2 hours.
- **Booking frequency:** One booking per day per person for longer sessions; no frequency limit for quick 15-minute chats.
- **Advance booking window:** 30 days.

## Why use LunaCal for booking limits and buffers?

LunaCal’s scheduling tool gives each event separate controls for preparation time, repeat bookings, and the booking window:

- **Allow different preparation and follow-up times.** Set before- and after-meeting buffers independently, so a short preparation period does not have to match a longer follow-up period.
- **Control repeat bookings by each attendee.** Per-person limits let you set a booking frequency for individual clients, useful for coaching sessions or other appointments that need a repeat-booking cap.
- **Change future booking rules without moving existing appointments.** Updates to advance notice and the booking window affect new bookings while leaving confirmed appointments unchanged.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I add a break between meetings?

In LunaCal, use **Buffer Before** or **Buffer After** to reserve time around an appointment. Set the two buffers independently to allow different amounts of time for preparation and follow-up; each supports up to 120 minutes.

### How much buffer time should I leave between appointments?

Start with 10 minutes before a meeting and 15 minutes after it, then adjust to the time you need for preparation, notes, or a break. LunaCal lets you set each buffer independently; these suggested values are optional.

### How do I stop last-minute bookings?

Set **Advance Notice** in LunaCal to require a minimum amount of time between making a booking and the appointment starting. Choose the required notice in minutes, hours, or days. This setting applies to new bookings and leaves existing appointments unchanged.

### How do I limit how often a client can book?

Use **Max Bookings per Event** in LunaCal to cap how often each person can book an event per day, week, month, or year. You can add up to four limit rules for an event.

### How do I control how far in advance people can book?

Set **Advance Booking Limit** in LunaCal to choose the future booking window or the exact days available for booking. Changing this setting affects new bookings; existing appointments remain unchanged.

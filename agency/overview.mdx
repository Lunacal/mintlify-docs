The agency workspace is where you run Lunacal under your own brand for the clients you manage. Your name, logo, domain, and email replace Lunacal's on the workspaces and booking pages those clients use.

Open it at [app.lunacal.ai/agency](https://app.lunacal.ai/agency).

## Who can use it

Agency is included with AppSumo tier 5 and tier 6. If your account does not include it, Lunacal sends you back to your event types when you open `/agency`.

A client of an agency cannot open this workspace, even if they can use the features you share with them. Clients stay in their own workspace. You manage them from yours.

## What you set up

The overview includes a **Setup checklist**. It is finished when all four items say **Configured**.

| Step | Where it lives | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Add your first client](/agency/clients) | **Clients**, or **Create client** | Gives you a client workspace to manage |
| [Branding](/agency/white-label) | **Settings → Agency Whitelabel** | Name, logo, favicon, support email, and legal links |
| [Custom domain](/agency/custom-domain) | **Settings → Custom Domain** | Serves the app and booking pages on your domain |
| [Custom email sending](/agency/email) | **Settings → Custom SMTP** | Sends mail from your own server |

You can do these in any order. The checklist highlights the next one that is still open and offers **Set up**.

Settings also has a **Billing** tab for the agency account. Workflow credits are not shown there.

## Where your brand shows up

After you save branding, clients and the people who book with them see your agency instead of Lunacal:

- The app name and logo
- The favicon
- The support email
- The Terms of Use and Privacy Policy links, when you have set them

A link you leave blank still points at Lunacal's own terms or privacy page. A warning on the branding page stays up until both links are filled in.

Brand color is on the form, but it is marked **Coming soon** and cannot be changed yet.

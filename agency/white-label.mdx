White label is the branding clients and the people who book with them see in place of Lunacal. Change it any time after setup.

Open **Settings** in the agency sidebar, then **Agency Whitelabel**: [app.lunacal.ai/agency/settings?tabName=agency-whitelabel](https://app.lunacal.ai/agency/settings?tabName=agency-whitelabel).

Settings opens on **Custom Domain** if you do not pick a tab. The other tabs are **Agency Whitelabel**, **Custom SMTP**, and **Billing**. The page heading is **Back**, and that control returns you to Overview.

The card heading is **Agency Whitelabel Branding**. The line under it is "Control the branding your clients and their bookers see."

**Save** stays disabled until you change something. A successful save says "Settings updated successfully". A failed save says something went wrong.

## Fields

| Field | Required | What you enter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Agency Name** | Yes | Placeholder "My Agency". Up to 100 characters. Empty is rejected with "Agency name is required". |
| **Logo** | Yes | Any crop. Image files only, up to 5 MB. Empty is rejected with "Logo is required". |
| **Favicon** | Yes | Square crop. Same file rules. Empty is rejected with "Favicon is required". |
| **Brand Color** | Shown, not editable | Marked **Coming Soon**. The picker and the presets under "Or pick a preset" are dimmed and cannot be clicked. |
| **Support Email** | Yes | Placeholder `support@youragency.com`. Must be a valid email. |
| **Privacy Policy URL** | No | Placeholder `https://youragency.com/privacy`. |
| **Terms of Use URL** | No | Placeholder `https://youragency.com/terms`. |

A file that is not an image shows "Please upload a valid image file (e.g., JPG, PNG, GIF)." A file over 5 MB shows "The image is too large. Maximum allowed size is 5MB." After a successful crop, **Change** replaces the image and **Remove** clears it.

**Brand Color** lists Indigo, Ocean, Emerald, and Sunset. None of them can be selected while the badge says **Coming Soon**. Saving keeps the color already stored. Setup stores Lunacal's default color because this step does not show the picker.

## Legal links

If either URL is empty, a warning stays on the card:

- Title: **Legal links incomplete**
- Message: "Your Privacy Policy and Terms of Use links aren't fully set. Any link you leave blank will show your clients and their bookers the default Terms of Use and Privacy Policy instead of your own."

Above the fields: "Shown to your clients and their bookers instead of the default Terms of Use and Privacy Policy."

A filled-in URL must start with `http://` or `https://`. Anything else shows "Enter a valid URL starting with http:// or https://". Clearing a URL and saving removes your link for that page only. The other link is unchanged. A missing link points at Lunacal's terms or privacy page.

## Where it shows up

Saved branding is used for your own agency view and for every client that belongs to you. That includes the app name, the logo and favicon (served as the product logo), the support email, and the Terms and Privacy links.

The checklist item **Branding** is **Configured** when the agency name and the logo are both saved. Favicon, support email, and the legal links are still required or warned about on this form, but they do not flip that checklist item by themselves.

Email uses this branding together with [your mail server](/agency/email).

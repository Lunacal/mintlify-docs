White label is the branding clients and bookers see in place of Lunacal. Change it any time after setup.

Open [Settings → Agency Whitelabel](https://app.lunacal.ai/agency/settings?tabName=agency-whitelabel).

The heading is **Agency Whitelabel Branding**. The line under it is "Control the branding your clients and their bookers see."

**Save** stays disabled until you change something. After a successful save, Lunacal confirms that settings were updated.

## What each field does

| Field | Required | What clients see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Agency Name** | Yes | The product name. Up to 100 characters. The placeholder is "My Agency". |
| **Logo** | Yes | The logo in the app. Crop it to any shape. |
| **Favicon** | Yes | The browser icon. Crop it to a square. |
| **Brand Color** | Shown, not editable | Marked **Coming Soon**. The swatches are Indigo, Ocean, Emerald, and Sunset, and they cannot be selected yet. |
| **Support Email** | Yes | The address clients use to reach you. The placeholder is `support@youragency.com`. |
| **Privacy Policy URL** | No | Replaces Lunacal's privacy link when it is a full `http://` or `https://` URL. |
| **Terms of Use URL** | No | Replaces Lunacal's terms link on the same rule. |

Images must be image files of 5 MB or less. If the upload fails, Lunacal shows an error and does not save that image.

## Legal links

Above the two URL fields, Lunacal explains that these links are shown instead of the default Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.

If either URL is empty, a warning stays on the page:

- Title: **Legal links incomplete**
- Message: any link you leave blank shows clients and bookers the default Terms of Use or Privacy Policy instead of yours.

A value that is not an `http://` or `https://` URL is rejected with a prompt to enter a valid URL. Clearing a URL and saving removes your link and restores the default for that one page. The other link is unchanged.

## Where this branding is used

Saved branding applies to your own agency view and to client workspaces that belong to you. That includes the shell of the app, booking pages, and the legal links in those experiences.

Email uses this branding together with [your SMTP settings](/agency/email). If you have not added SMTP, mail still goes out, but through Lunacal's mail server.

The checklist item **Branding** is complete when the agency name and logo are saved. The description on the checklist is "App name and logo".

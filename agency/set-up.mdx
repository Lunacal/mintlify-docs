Setup is a two-step page. The left side says **Launch your agency** and lists the steps **Set up your agency** and **Domain & email**. You can click a step to move between them.

Open it from **Setup Agency** in the workspace switcher, or go to [app.lunacal.ai/agency/setup](https://app.lunacal.ai/agency/setup). The **X** in the corner is titled "Go to agency workspace" and takes you to Overview. Anything you already saved is filled in again the next time you open setup.

## Step 1. Set up your agency

The heading is **Set up your agency**. Under it: "Your name and logo appear on your clients' booking pages."

<Steps>
  <Step title="Agency Name">
    **Agency Name** is required. The placeholder is "Northwind". The name can be up to 100 characters. Leave it empty and Lunacal shows **Required** under the field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Logo and Favicon">
    Both fields show a required asterisk.

    - **Logo** crops to any shape. The empty box is wide and says **+ Upload**.
    - **Favicon** crops to a square.

    Use an image file. Lunacal rejects other files with "Please upload a valid image file (e.g., JPG, PNG, GIF)." Files over 5 MB get "The image is too large. Maximum allowed size is 5MB."

    After you pick a file, crop it and confirm. **Change** replaces the image. **Remove** clears it.

    Saving without a logo or favicon is rejected. The messages are "Logo is required" and "Favicon is required".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Support Email">
    **Support Email** is required. The placeholder is `support@youragency.com`. An address without a valid email shape shows "Please enter a valid email".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Legal links">
    If **Privacy Policy URL** or **Terms of Use URL** is empty, a warning stays on the page:

    - Title: **Legal links incomplete**
    - Message: any link you leave blank shows clients and bookers the default Terms of Use and Privacy Policy instead of yours.

    The line under the warning says these links are shown instead of the default Terms of Use and Privacy Policy. Placeholders are `https://youragency.com/privacy` and `https://youragency.com/terms`.

    A value that does not start with `http://` or `https://` shows "Enter a valid URL starting with http:// or https://".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Next Step">
    Click **Next Step**. Lunacal saves the branding and opens **Domain & email**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Brand color is not on this step. Lunacal stores its default color so the save can succeed. You cannot pick a color. On Settings the picker is visible and marked **Coming Soon**. See [Brand the workspace](/agency/white-label).

## Step 2. Domain and email

The heading is **Connect your domain & email**. Under it: "Add your custom domain and sending email so your clients see your brand everywhere."

Two sections. Each one shows **Not set up**, **In progress**, **Connected**, or **Setup failed**.

| Section | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Agency Domain** | "Host your white-labeled app on your own domain." |
| **Agency SMTP** | "Send booking emails from your own mailbox." |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Agency Domain">
    The field is **Your custom domain**, placeholder `app.youragency.com`. Click **Set up domain**. The DNS records and **Verify DNS** appear on the same page. The steps are on [Use your own domain](/agency/custom-domain).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Agency SMTP">
    Fill in the mail server and click **Save SMTP**. After it is saved, the button reads **Update SMTP**. The password placeholder becomes "•••••••• (unchanged)" when you come back. The steps are on [Send email from your domain](/agency/email).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Leave">
    **Finish** opens the agency workspace. **Skip for now** does the same thing.

    You do not have to wait for DNS. The note beside **Verify DNS** says CDN propagation can take up to an hour, and that you can continue and finish later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The same branding, domain, and email settings are under **Settings** after you leave. A domain you create here is not the agency domain until you choose **Make agency domain** on [Settings → Custom Domain](/agency/custom-domain).

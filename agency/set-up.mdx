The first time you launch the agency, Lunacal opens a short setup. You can leave it and come back. Anything you already saved is filled in again.

Open [app.lunacal.ai/agency/setup](https://app.lunacal.ai/agency/setup). The close button in the corner takes you to the agency overview. You can finish domain and email later from Settings.

## Step 1. Set up your agency

The page title is **Set up your agency**. The description under it is "Your name and logo appear on your clients' booking pages."

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name the agency">
    **Agency Name** is required. The placeholder is "Northwind". Use the name clients should see in the product. It can be up to 100 characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a logo and a favicon">
    Both are required.

    - **Logo** can be cropped to any shape.
    - **Favicon** is cropped to a square.

    Use an image file, up to 5 MB. Lunacal rejects other file types. After you pick a file, crop it, then confirm. **Change** replaces the image. You can remove it and upload again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a support email">
    **Support Email** is required. Use an address your clients can write to, such as `support@youragency.com`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your legal links, if you have them">
    **Privacy Policy URL** and **Terms of Use URL** are optional. Each one must start with `http://` or `https://` if you fill it in.

    If either box is empty, the page warns you that clients and bookers will see Lunacal's page for the one you left blank.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue">
    Save the step. Lunacal moves you to **Domain & email**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Brand color is not on this step. Lunacal stores a default color so the rest of the form can save. You cannot pick a color yet. See [Brand the workspace](/agency/white-label).

## Step 2. Domain and email

The page title is **Connect your domain & email**. Under it: "Add your custom domain and sending email so your clients see your brand everywhere."

Two sections sit on the page. The steps along the top call this step **Domain & email**. Each section shows one of these statuses: **Not set up**, **In progress**, **Connected**, or **Setup failed**.

| Section | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Agency Domain** | "Host your white-labeled app on your own domain." |
| **Agency SMTP** | "Send booking emails from your own mailbox." |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the domain, if you have DNS access now">
    Open **Agency Domain**, enter the hostname, and click **Set up domain**. The records and the **Verify DNS** button appear on the same page. Full steps are on [Use your own domain](/agency/custom-domain).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the mail server">
    Open **Agency SMTP** and click **Save SMTP**. After it is saved, the button reads **Update SMTP**. Full steps are on [Send email from your domain](/agency/email).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Leave the setup">
    Click **Finish** to open the agency workspace. **Skip for now** does the same thing if you want to add the domain or the mail server later.

    DNS does not have to finish first. The page says CDN propagation can take up to an hour, and that you can continue and finish later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The same settings are under [Agency settings](https://app.lunacal.ai/agency/settings) after you leave this page. A domain you create here still has to be marked as the agency domain from **Settings → Custom Domain** before it serves the agency app. That step is on [Use your own domain](/agency/custom-domain).

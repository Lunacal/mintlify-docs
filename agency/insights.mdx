**Insights** is the same bookings dashboard you use on a personal account, limited to your clients. The AI Agent tab is not on this page.

Open it from the sidebar or at [app.lunacal.ai/agency/insights](https://app.lunacal.ai/agency/insights).

The heading is **Insights**. The subtitle is "Bookings, customers and revenue across all your clients."

## Tabs

| Tab | Address |
| --- | --- |
| **Insights** | `/agency/insights?tab=insights` |
| **Bookings** | `/agency/insights?tab=bookings` |
| **Customers** | `/agency/insights?tab=customers` |

**Insights** is selected when you open the page. An unknown `tab` value is replaced with `insights`.

On **Customers**, choosing a person adds their email to the address so you can share that view. Closing the person removes the email and returns you to the customer list.

## Before you have clients

With no clients, the page shows **No clients yet** and "Insights will appear here once you create a client and they start receiving bookings." **Create client** starts the same wizard as [Clients](/agency/clients).

Filters on this page only include your client workspaces. They do not include your personal account.

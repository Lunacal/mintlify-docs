**Licenses** lists every person your clients have added to their workspaces. The page subtitle is "Everyone your clients have added to their workspaces. Each person uses one license."

Open it from the sidebar, from the **Licenses used** tile on Overview, or at [app.lunacal.ai/agency/licenses](https://app.lunacal.ai/agency/licenses).

## The summary bar

Four cells sit across the top. The last three are filters. Click a cell to show only those people. Click it again to clear the filter.

| Cell | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Licenses used** | Used / total, how many are still available, and a meter. This cell does not filter. |
| **Pending Invites** | Invites that have not been accepted. The hint is "Pending invites don't use a license until accepted." |
| **Inactive members** | Accepted people with no bookings in the last 30 days and none upcoming. |
| **Calendar issues** | Accepted people with no calendar, or with a calendar that is disconnected. |

## Find someone

**Search by client, name or email** matches the client name, the person's name, their email, and their username.

The status menu is **All**, **Active members**, **Pending invites**, **Inactive members**, and **Calendar issues**. **Clear filters** appears once a search or a filter is hiding rows. If nothing matches, the page says so and offers **Clear filters**.

## Each client

Clients are grouped. The header shows the client name, how many licenses are in use, bookings in the last 30 days, and a pending count when someone has not accepted. Collapse the group with the chevron. The external-link button opens that client's **Members** page.

A client with nobody yet says "This client has no members yet."

Each person has these columns:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Member** | Name and email. Tags can include **Client contact**, **Pending**, **Hidden**, and **Scheduling off**. |
| **Role** | Their role on that client |
| **Event types** | How many event types they have |
| **Bookings (30d)** | Bookings in the last 30 days |
| **Upcoming** | Upcoming bookings |
| **Calendar** | **No calendar**, **Calendar disconnected**, or how many are connected |

Open the row for more: timezone, the date they joined, next booking, last booking, personal event types, connected calendars, and assigned event types.

## What you can do from the row menu

| Action | When it shows | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **View as client** | The person has a username | Signs you in as them. The confirm dialog says you can act on their behalf and return from the banner at the top. |
| **Edit role** | They have accepted | Changes their role on that client |
| **Hide from booking page** or **Unhide from booking page** | They have accepted | Shows or hides them on the booking page |
| **Disable member scheduling** or **Enable member scheduling** | They have accepted | Turns their scheduling off or back on |
| **Resend invitation** | The invite is still pending | Sends the invitation again. Lunacal confirms the invitation was resent. |
| **Release license** | Always | Removes them from that client and frees the license |

**Release license** asks you to confirm. The message says they will be removed from this client and their license will be freed. If they do not belong to any other workspace, their account is deleted. After it succeeds, Lunacal says the license was released.

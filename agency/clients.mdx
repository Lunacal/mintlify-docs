A client is a workspace you manage from the agency. Their app and booking pages use your [branding](/agency/white-label), [domain](/agency/custom-domain), and [email](/agency/email).

Open **Clients** in the agency sidebar, or go to [app.lunacal.ai/agency/clients](https://app.lunacal.ai/agency/clients).

The heading is **Clients**. The subtitle is "Create, manage, and switch into your clients' workspaces." **Create client** is in the page header.

## Create a client

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create client">
    Click **Create client**. Lunacal opens **Create a new client**.

    With no clients yet, the empty state heading is **No clients yet**. The line under it is "Create your first client to start managing their workspace from here." It offers **Create client** as well. The setup checklist uses the same link, under **Add your first client**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish the two-step wizard">
    This is the workspace wizard, with client wording. Complete both steps. Lunacal opens that client's workspace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You cannot invite another agency owner into a client workspace. Lunacal rejects that invite so two agencies are not set as each other's owner.

## Find a client

**Search clients** matches the client name and the booking slug. The sort menu is **Name** (the default), **Most bookings**, **Newest**, and **Needs attention**.

**All** and **Needs attention** sit beside the sort. Each shows a count. **Needs attention** keeps clients that have one of these notes under their name:

| Note | Why it is there |
| --- | --- |
| **No event types setup** | The client has no event types |
| **No members** | The client has no members |
| **No booking domain** | The client has no booking domain |
| **No upcoming bookings** | They have event types, and nothing is upcoming |

If the search or the filter hides everyone, the page says no clients match and offers **Clear filters**.

## Read a row

Click the name to open that client's workspace. Under the name you see their booking hostname, or `/their-slug`, or **No booking domain**. A shield marks a live custom domain. When they have a booking link, the copy button is **Copy booking link**.

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Client** | Name, booking address, and attention notes |
| **Status** | **Healthy**, **Needs setup**, or **Inactive** |
| **Members** | How many, plus a pending count when invites are open |
| **Event types** | How many event types |
| **Bookings (30d)** | Bookings in the last 30 days, with the change versus the previous 30 days |
| **Upcoming** | Upcoming bookings |
| **Last booking** | How long ago the last booking was |

The counts above the table use the same three status labels.

| Status | When you see it |
| --- | --- |
| **Needs setup** | No event types, no members, or no booking domain |
| **Inactive** | Setup is done, and they have no upcoming bookings and none in the last 30 days |
| **Healthy** | Setup is done, and they have an upcoming booking or a booking in the last 30 days |

## Row menu

| Action | When it shows | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Set up client** | You are setting the account up yourself, and they have not been handed the login yet | Signs you into their onboarding at **Getting started** so you can finish their profile and event types |
| **Inform client** | Same, and only the first time | Opens the handoff dialog |
| **Open workspace** | Always | Opens the client workspace |
| **Members** | Always | Opens that client's members |
| **View as client** | The primary member has a username | Asks you to confirm, then signs you in as them. The dialog says you can return to your agency account from the banner at the top. |
| **Delete client** | Always | Permanently deletes the client |

**Delete client** uses the confirm button **Yes, delete client**. The message is "This permanently deletes the client and removes all their members and event types. This can't be undone."

## Hand the client their login

**Inform client** is the handoff. The description is "Set a password and email the client their login details. They'll be asked to verify their email and finish onboarding the first time they sign in."

The dialog shows **Login email** (you can copy it) and **Temporary password**. Lunacal fills in a password each time you open the dialog. Edit it, or use **Generate a new password** to replace it. The hint under the field is "Share this password with your client. They can change it after signing in." **Send credentials** stays disabled until the password is at least 8 characters. **Cancel** closes the dialog without sending.

**Send credentials** confirms **Credentials sent to the client**. The email subject is "Your {workspace} account is ready." It includes the login email, the temporary password, and a **Sign in** link. The link uses your agency domain when that domain is live, and Lunacal's app address when it is not.

The first sign-in asks them to verify their email and finish their profile. **Inform client** is not offered again from this menu after that first send.

A client is a workspace you manage from the agency. Their booking pages and app use your [branding](/agency/white-label), [domain](/agency/custom-domain), and [email](/agency/email).

Open [Clients](https://app.lunacal.ai/agency/clients). The page subtitle is "Create, manage, and switch into your clients' workspaces."

## Create a client

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start from Clients">
    Click **Create client**. Lunacal opens **Create a new client**.

    If you have no clients yet, the empty state says "Create your first client to start managing their workspace from here" and offers the same button. The setup checklist item is **Add your first client**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish the workspace wizard">
    This is the same two-step workspace wizard Lunacal uses for a team, with client wording. Complete both steps. Lunacal returns you to that client's workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to Clients">
    The new client is listed on [Clients](https://app.lunacal.ai/agency/clients). Search by name. Sort by name, newest, most bookings in the last 30 days, or who needs attention.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Read a client's status

Each client has a health label.

| Label | When you see it |
| --- | --- |
| **Needs setup** | The client has no event types, no members, or no booking domain |
| **Inactive** | Setup is done, and they have no upcoming bookings and none in the last 30 days |
| **Healthy** | Setup is done and they have an upcoming booking or a booking in the last 30 days |

A client can also be flagged when they have event types but nothing upcoming. Use **Needs attention** in the sort if you want those rows first.

## Set the account up yourself

When you are the one preparing the client's account, open the **⋯** menu on their row.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up client">
    **Set up client** signs you into that client's onboarding so you can add their event types and profile. You are acting as the client until you leave that session.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Inform client">
    **Inform client** is how you hand the account over. The dialog shows their login email and a **Temporary password**. Lunacal suggests a password. You can edit it before sending. Copy either value if you also want to share it yourself.

    The dialog says they will be asked to verify their email and finish onboarding the first time they sign in.

    Send it. Lunacal confirms **Credentials sent to the client**. The email includes the login email, the temporary password, and a sign-in link. That link uses your domain when your domain is live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Inform client** is offered once, the first time you hand over the account. The clients list does not show a second send. The first sign-in asks the client to verify their email and complete their profile.

You cannot invite another agency owner into a client workspace. Lunacal rejects that invite so two agencies are not set as each other's owner.
